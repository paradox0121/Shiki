Shiki — Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to sachinmukherjee05@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

I bought something and it's missing

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, or the same link on the Golden flowers tab of the garden shop. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

My progress is gone

Progress, flowers and items you earned are saved on your device only. Deleting the app erases them. Purchases made with real money can always be restored.

There's no sound

Tap the screen once on the title. Check that the sound and music buttons at the top of the game aren't crossed out, and turn up your volume.

Can I play without ads?

Shiki never shows ads on its own. The only ads are the two you can choose to watch: to continue after a fall, and to double the flowers from a level. If you’d rather skip those too, Skip the ads in the garden shop lets you continue and double your flowers without watching anything.
