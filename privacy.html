Shiki — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Shiki is made and published by Sachin Mukherjee, an independent developer (“we” below). It is a game that keeps your progress on your own device. We do not run accounts or servers of our own. The game can show ads that you choose to watch, sells optional items, and on iPhone it can use Apple’s Game Center. Those features use the services described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, settings, flowers, golden flowers and the items you own are saved in local storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the app erases it.

Advertising

The only ads are ones you choose to watch for a reward (to continue after a fall, or to double the flowers from a level). They are shown through Google AdMob (in the apps) or Google AdSense (on the website). To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, you will be asked whether ads may use tracking; the game works the same either way. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EU and UK), you will be asked for consent first and can change it later. Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

Game Center (iPhone)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Endless garden score and the achievements you unlock are sent to Apple’s Game Center so they appear on its leaderboard and in your profile. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we do not receive your Game Center details. You can sign out in your iPhone’s Settings → Game Center.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases, the apps use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Purchases made with real money can be restored from your app store account with “Restore purchases”.

What we do not do

  • No accounts of our own and no sign-in to us.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Children

Shiki is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the ad or purchase services, contact us and we will help you request its deletion from those providers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
